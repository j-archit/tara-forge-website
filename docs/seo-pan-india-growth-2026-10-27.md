## Service-page brainstorm — proposals, not published pages

These are hypotheses based on the current portfolio, not measured keyword volumes or a promise of rankings. The selected commercial priorities are **gifts and small batches**, as confirmed by the owner. Functional parts/prototypes are a later option.

| Proposed page | Customer question it should answer | Existing evidence | Information to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Custom gifts and display models | Can you print my figurine, decorative piece or display model and ship it to me? | Lotus ring holder, One Piece gift, carriage, vase and display models | Which file-preparation/design help is offered, finishing options and project examples suitable for publication |
| Functional parts and prototypes | Can you print this replacement part, enclosure or fit-check prototype in a suitable material? | Existing PLA/PETG services and printability-review process | Actual functional-project photos, intended use and tested limitations; display models should not stand in for functional-performance evidence |
| Small-batch 3D printing | Can you make a consistent batch for my club, event or small business? | Bangalore Chess Club bag tags and its existing review | Actual quantity, material, consistency checks and agreed production schedule |

Each page should explain what can be printed, what files/details to send, relevant material choices, finishing, quote requirements and shipping. Use specific project photos and link directly to the quote flow and shipping policy. Possible paths are `/services/custom-gifts-display-models/`, `/services/functional-parts-prototypes/` and `/services/small-batch-printing/`; final names should follow the selected scope.

Shared factual limits: quotes within 24 hours; production timing depends on final files, confirmed quote, print time and receipt of advance; shipping is charged separately at actual carrier cost. Typical 48-hour production is conditional and is not a delivery promise. Custom design work is discussed and quoted, not automatically included.

## Customer-story brainstorm — proposals, not new testimonials

| Story | Useful angle | Evidence available | Missing details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lotus ring holder | A decorative gift printed for a Delhi customer | Existing views and Divyansh's approved testimonial | Dimensions, material, finishing and customer brief |
| One Piece display gift | Personalised display printing and interstate shipping | Existing photos and approved testimonial mentioning Delhi/Bangalore and safe shipping | Confirmed project specifications and the brief; retain its display-piece classification |
| Bangalore Chess Club bag tags | Repeatable printing for a club batch | Existing testimonial and public Google review/photos | Quantity, material and any production details to discuss publicly |
| Horse-drawn carriage | Detail across an assembled horse-and-carriage model | Two preprocessed gallery views; email says delivered 27 October 2026 | Intended use, dimensions, material, destination and assembly/finishing scope |

A useful first story can be short: customer need, agreed print requirements, relevant preparation/finishing, finished views, then delivery experience and an existing approved review where applicable. Leave unknown facts out until confirmed. Each story should link to the relevant service page and quote action. The jet-engine display is labelled personal use on the site and should remain portfolio evidence rather than being presented as a customer order.

Recommended first release: two service pages, one for custom gifts/display models and one for small-batch printing. Support the gifts page with the ring-holder and One Piece stories; support the small-batch page with the Chess Club bag-tag story. Build the functional/prototype story later, once a suitable real project can substantiate it.

The gifts page should help an individual buyer understand file/design requirements, size, finish, the approval process and shipping for a personal gift. The small-batch page should help a club, organiser or small business discuss quantity, repeatability, custom branding where applicable and a required date. Both should request the delivery destination when starting a quote. Confirm quantity discounts and batch capacity per project; no fixed minimum order, discount percentage or guaranteed turnaround has been established for publication.

## Evaluating results

After deployment, request recrawling of the changed pages in Search Console and retain the deployment date as a comparison point. Compare branded, local-intent and general service queries separately. Query intent is not a reliable measure of the searcher's city: track actual enquiry and delivery destinations as well. Choose the first service page based on desired work and the evidence available for it, then assess qualified enquiries as well as impressions and clicks.
