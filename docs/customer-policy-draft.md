# Remaining customer policy decisions — draft for owner review

The public shipping, returns and refunds page covers the terms the owner has confirmed. The recommendations below are not published policy. Decide the remaining operational details and have final wording reviewed for applicable Indian consumer law before adding it to the website or individual quotes.

## Agree on each quote before taking the advance

The written quote should specify the final file version, material, colour, quantity, approximate dimensions, finish, price, shipping charge, and estimated production and dispatch dates. State whether design work or revisions are included. Confirm these details with the customer before requesting the 50% UPI advance.

## Suggested rules

1. **Changes:** Changes before production starts can be discussed and re-quoted. Changes after printing starts may require new material, print time, and a revised schedule. Obtain the customer's approval before charging more or restarting production.
2. **Cancellation:** Before production starts, offer a refund of the advance, subtracting only separately agreed design work already completed, if any. After production starts, provide an itemised account of actual work and materials already used and refund any unused amount. Avoid a blanket “non-refundable advance” statement.
3. **Print defects or mismatch:** The public page invites prompt contact with photos and, if available, an unboxing video; it says verified claims are reviewed case by case. Decide internally when to correct, reprint, or refund. A reporting window should not purport to remove statutory rights.
4. **Customer files:** Review obvious printability issues before confirmation. Record any known limitations or tolerances in the quote. Do not promise that a print will meet unspecified fit, strength, or use requirements.
5. **Shipping and delays:** The public page says pan-India shipping is charged separately at actual carrier cost and confirmed before dispatch. Agree an estimated dispatch date with each customer. If a committed schedule slips, contact them with a revised date and a fair cancellation or refund option where appropriate.

## Decisions to make before publication

- Who pays return shipping for a confirmed defect or damage in transit?
- Is there a suggested period for reporting a problem, while preserving legal rights?
- What counts as included design work, and how is its cost approved?
- Will you offer any voluntary fit or durability warranty beyond the agreed specification?
- How will refunds be sent? The public page promises processing within 2–4 business days after a claim is verified.
- Who receives complaints and how will customers reach them?

The [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020](https://thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Rules/Consumer%20Protection%20%28E-Commerce%29%20Rules%2C%202020.pdf) call for clear information on refunds, shipping, payment modes, and redressal, and address defective or misdescribed goods. The exact obligations for this email-led workflow and any later online checkout should be checked when the policy is finalised.
